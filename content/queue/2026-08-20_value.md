## Caption
That patient who "was going to call back"? They booked with someone else by Tuesday.

The fix isn't hiring a receptionist. It's one message that goes out the second a lead goes quiet — and gets them back on your schedule without you lifting a finger.

We use this exact follow-up prompt to turn cold leads into booked appointments for dental offices, salons, and every business that lives on the calendar.

Comment PROMPT and it's in your DMs.

## Prompt payload (ManyChat DM)
THE "SILENT LEAD" RECOVERY SCRIPT
Use this when a lead inquired, got a quote, or booked a consult — then went quiet for 2+ days.

STEP 1 — THE PROMPT (paste into ChatGPT or your AI assistant):

"You are writing a short, warm follow-up message from [YOUR BUSINESS NAME], a [YOUR BUSINESS TYPE, e.g. dental office / salon / photography studio]. This message is going to a lead named [LEAD NAME] who [reached out / booked a consult / requested a quote] on [DATE] about [SERVICE, e.g. teeth whitening / bridal hair trial / a family portrait session] and has not responded since.

Write a text message (under 300 characters) that:
- Feels personal, not automated
- Reminds them what they asked about, specifically
- Removes friction by offering one easy next step (a link, a time, or a one-word reply)
- Has zero guilt or pressure — sounds like a helpful human, not a chaser
- Ends with a low-effort question they can answer in 3 words or less

Then write a second, slightly more direct version to send only if there's still no reply after 48 hours — same warmth, but with one added line naming a specific benefit of acting now (e.g. limited openings this week, a seasonal offer, or a real scarcity point relevant to my business)."

STEP 2 — THE MINI WORKFLOW (how to actually run this without babysitting it):
1. Every new lead lands in one place — a hosted form, DM, or call log.
2. If there's no reply in 48 hours, this exact prompt auto-generates message #1 and sends it by text.
3. If there's still no reply in another 48 hours, message #2 sends automatically.
4. Any reply — even one word — pings your phone so you can jump in personally.

This is the same logic Strumode installs inside a business's SMS command line: leads never go cold, and the owner never has to remember who they forgot to text back.

Swap in your business type, service, and lead name each time — or let it run on autopilot once it's built into your system.
