Getting reviews shouldn't depend on remembering to ask, or hoping someone feels like typing one out unprompted.

## Prompt payload (ManyChat DM)
THE 5-STAR REVIEW REQUEST SYSTEM
A ready-to-use text/email script + mini-workflow for getting more reviews without feeling pushy.

STEP 1 — TIMING
Send this within 1-2 hours of a purchase, appointment, or job completion — while the good feeling is still fresh. Never wait until the next day.

STEP 2 — THE MESSAGE (copy and personalize the bracketed parts)

"Hey [First Name], it's [Your Name] from [Business Name]. So glad you [came in today / picked up your order / got your new ___]. Hope you're loving it.

If you have 30 seconds, a quick review would mean a lot to us — it's how other people find us. Here's the link: [Google/Yelp review link]

Thank you for supporting a small business. Truly."

STEP 3 — THE FOLLOW-UP (if no response after 3 days)
"Hi [First Name], no pressure at all — just floating this back up in case it got buried. Here's that review link again if you get a free minute: [link]. Either way, thanks for being a customer."

STEP 4 — HANDLING A LESS-THAN-HAPPY CUSTOMER
Before sending the review request, ask this filter question first: "On a scale of 1-5, how was your experience today?"
- If they say 4 or 5 → send the review request above.
- If they say 1-3 → do NOT ask for a public review. Instead reply: "Thank you for being honest. What can we do to make this right?" Handle it privately first.

STEP 5 — AUTOMATE IT
This entire sequence (the ask, the filter question, the follow-up, and routing unhappy customers away from public review links) is exactly the kind of thing Strumode wires into your system so it fires automatically after every sale — no one on your team has to remember to send it.
