## Caption
Every small business owner knows this feeling: the business is growing, but somehow you're the one drowning.

Picture Maria. Her bakery is busy — the good kind of busy. Custom orders rolling in from texts, DMs, walk-ins. But nothing was in one place. So she carried it all in her head, her notes app, and a growing pile of guilt about the reply she forgot to send.

Then one order slipped through. Not a small one.

That's the moment most owners realize the problem isn't effort. It's structure. Strumode installs the system behind the scenes — the intake form, the automatic deposit request, the reminders, the dashboard that shows every order in one glance. Nothing rides on memory anymore.

Maria didn't hire anyone. She just stopped being the system.
