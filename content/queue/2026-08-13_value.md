## Prompt payload (ManyChat DM)
THE "COME BACK AND BUY" FOLLOW-UP SCRIPT
For boutiques, retail shops, and any business with in-person or online browsers who don't buy on the spot.

HOW TO USE IT:
Copy the prompt below into ChatGPT (or any AI chat tool). Fill in the bracketed details about the customer and what they looked at. It writes a text/email you can send within a few hours of the visit — while it's still fresh for them.

THE PROMPT:

"You are a warm, low-pressure sales assistant for a small [type of shop, e.g. boutique/bakery/gift shop] called [business name]. A customer named [first name] came in or browsed online and looked at [specific item(s) they liked, e.g. 'the navy wrap dress and a pair of gold earrings'] but didn't purchase.

Write a short, friendly follow-up message (text message length, under 400 characters) that:
1. References the specific item(s) by name, not generically
2. Sounds like a real person who remembers them, not an automated blast
3. Offers one small, genuine reason to come back — new stock, a fit note, a limited quantity, or a simple 'let me know if you have questions' — do NOT invent a discount or promotion unless I tell you one exists
4. Ends with a low-pressure question, not a hard sell
5. Has zero exclamation-point overload — one, max, if it fits naturally

Then give me a second, shorter version (under 160 characters) formatted for SMS.

Do not use the words 'act now,' 'limited time,' or 'don't miss out.' Keep the tone like a helpful shop owner, not a marketing department."

WHY THIS WORKS:
Specificity beats speed. A message that names the exact item outperforms a generic "come back soon!" because it proves a human noticed them. Sent within a few hours, before the interest cools, it turns a maybe into a sale without ever sounding pushy.

THE NEXT LEVEL:
This works even better when it sends itself — the moment a lead fills out your form or a customer's info lands in your system, triggered automatically, no sticky notes, no forgetting.
