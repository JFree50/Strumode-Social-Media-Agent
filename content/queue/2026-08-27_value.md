## Caption
Every boutique owner has that customer who messages at 11pm asking "did my order ship?" — and then forgets to answer for two days.

We built a reply script that handles order questions, restock questions, and return questions in your exact tone, in seconds. Copy it into your phone, your DMs, or your automation tool and stop losing sleep over messages.

Comment PROMPT and it's in your DMs.

## Prompt payload (ManyChat DM)
CUSTOMER MESSAGE REPLY SCRIPT — for boutiques, shops & e-commerce sellers

Use this prompt in ChatGPT (or paste customer messages into your Strumode command line for auto-drafted replies).

---
PROMPT:

"You are helping me reply to customer messages for my shop, [BUSINESS NAME]. My tone is [warm and casual / polished and professional / friendly and quick — pick one]. 

Here is the customer's message:
'[PASTE MESSAGE HERE]'

Here is what I know about their situation:
- Order status: [e.g. shipped 2 days ago / not yet shipped / delivered]
- Product involved: [item name]
- Any relevant policy: [e.g. returns accepted within 14 days, restocks happen every Friday]

Write a short reply (3–5 sentences) that:
1. Acknowledges their question directly — no generic 'thanks for reaching out' filler.
2. Gives them a clear, specific answer using the details above.
3. Offers one helpful next step (tracking link, restock date, return instructions, etc.).
4. Ends warmly, in my tone, without sounding scripted or robotic.

Do not apologize excessively. Do not use exclamation points more than once. Keep it human."

---
HOW TO USE IT DAILY:
- Keep this prompt saved in your Notes app or DM drafts.
- Swap in the customer's message and your order details each time — takes 20 seconds.
- If you're a Strumode client, this exact logic runs automatically: customer messages get triaged and drafted for you before you even open your phone.

TIP: Save 3 versions of this — one for order status, one for returns, one for restock questions — so you're never starting from a blank page.
