## Caption
Every small business owner knows this feeling: the shop runs fine, but you don't.

Picture Priya. Boutique owner. Great eye for product, no time left for herself. Every night after close, she's answering DMs about restocks, chasing down a supplier invoice, texting a customer back about alterations — the real work of running the place happens after the lights go off.

Nothing was broken. It just all lived in her head, her phone, her notebook. Until one slow Tuesday she missed a DM from a customer ready to buy — and the sale went to a shop that answered faster.

That was the moment she stopped doing admin manually and let a system do it instead. One hosted form to catch every lead. Instant alerts so nothing sits unread. Invoices that send and follow up on their own. A single dashboard to see it all — and a text line to run it from her couch.

Priya didn't get a new business. She got her nights back.
