## Caption
Every small bakery owner knows this feeling.

You started the business to bake. Somehow you ended up running a call center, an accounts department, and a scheduling desk too — all before 6am.

Meet Elena. She owns a neighborhood bakery. Her croissants sell out by 9am. Her nights don't end when the ovens cool — they end when she's done replying to custom cake orders, chasing invoices, and double-checking she didn't miss a wedding order buried in her texts.

This is the story of what changes when the admin runs itself.
