## Prompt payload (ManyChat DM)
THE MISSED-LEAD RECOVERY TEXT — copy this into ChatGPT (or any AI chat tool) and fill in the brackets:

---
You are writing a short text message (SMS) from a small business owner to a potential customer who reached out but hasn't heard back yet. The tone is warm, direct, and personal — like a busy owner texting between jobs, not a company.

Business type: [e.g. plumbing company, hair studio, dog groomer]
Customer's original request: [what they asked about]
How long it's been: [e.g. "2 days"]
What you want them to do next: [e.g. book a call, reply with a good time, confirm they still need the service]

Write 3 versions:
1. A short casual check-in (under 25 words)
2. A slightly more detailed one that re-states what they asked for, so they feel heard
3. A "last chance" follow-up for if they still don't reply after version 1 or 2

Rules:
- No corporate language ("per our previous conversation," "we regret to inform")
- No exclamation-point salesmanship
- Sound like a real person, not a script
- End each version with an easy yes/no or one-tap action, not an open-ended question
---

HOW TO USE IT:
Run this every time a lead goes quiet for 24–48 hours. Send version 1 first. If no reply in 2 days, send version 2. If still nothing after a week, send version 3 — it's your permission-to-close-the-loop message, and it often gets the best response because it removes pressure.
