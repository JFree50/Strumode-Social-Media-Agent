## Caption
Every boutique owner has a pile of "still thinking about it" customers who never came back to buy.

You don't need a sale to bring them back. You need the right message at the right moment.

We use a 3-message win-back sequence that turns cold browsers into paying customers — without sounding like a discount-code spam bot.

Comment PROMPT and it's in your DMs

## Prompt payload (ManyChat DM)
THE 3-MESSAGE CUSTOMER WIN-BACK SEQUENCE (for boutiques, shops, and any business with past customers who went quiet)

Use this with any customer who bought once, browsed and left, or hasn't ordered in 60+ days. Send as email or text, spaced out over 7 days.

SETUP — fill in your details first:
- [BUSINESS NAME]
- [CUSTOMER FIRST NAME]
- [LAST ITEM THEY BOUGHT OR BROWSED]
- [A NEW ITEM OR RESTOCK THEY'D LIKE]
- [YOUR BOOKING/SHOP LINK]

---

MESSAGE 1 (Day 1 — no ask, just warmth):
"Hey [CUSTOMER FIRST NAME], it's [BUSINESS NAME]. You picked up [LAST ITEM] a while back and we just wanted to check in — hope you're still loving it. We've got some new pieces in that reminded us of your taste. No pressure, just wanted to say hi."

MESSAGE 2 (Day 4 — the reason to come back):
"We just brought in [NEW ITEM OR RESTOCK] and thought of you right away. If you want first pick before we post it publicly, reply here or shop early: [SHOP LINK]"

MESSAGE 3 (Day 7 — light urgency, easy exit):
"Last note from us on this one — [NEW ITEM] is almost gone and we didn't want you to miss it. If now's not the time, no worries at all, we'll see you next time you're in. [SHOP LINK]"
