## Prompt payload (ManyChat DM)
THE NO-AWKWARDNESS INVOICE FOLLOW-UP SYSTEM

Use this any time an invoice is more than 3 days late. Copy the prompt below into ChatGPT (or any AI chat tool), fill in the brackets, and send what it gives you.

---

PROMPT TO PASTE:

"Write a short, friendly but direct payment follow-up message for a client. Details:

- Client name: [NAME]
- What the invoice was for: [SERVICE/JOB]
- Amount due: [$ AMOUNT]
- How many days late: [X DAYS]
- My tone: [warm and professional / casual and direct — pick one]
- Payment link or method: [LINK OR INSTRUCTIONS]

Rules:
- Keep it under 60 words.
- No guilt-tripping, no over-explaining.
- Assume it's an honest oversight, not a dispute.
- End with a clear, easy next step.
- Write 3 versions: one for text message, one for email, one for a phone call script."

---

WHY THIS WORKS:
Most late payments aren't disputes — they're forgotten. This prompt keeps your ask short, assumes good faith, and makes paying you the easiest option on the table. Use the text version first. If no reply in 3 days, send the email. Still nothing after a week, use the call script.

Pro tip: The businesses that never chase invoices at all usually aren't doing this by hand — they've got the reminder, the pay link, and the follow-up running automatically the moment a due date passes.
