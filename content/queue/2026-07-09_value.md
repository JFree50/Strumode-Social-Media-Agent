## Caption
Every missed call is a patient who books with someone else.

Most small offices lose appointments not because service is bad, but because nobody answers fast enough. There's a simple script that turns a missed call into a booked slot — before the patient even thinks about calling your competitor.

We use a version of this inside the Strumode system to auto-reply and rebook leads in minutes, not hours.

Want the exact script?

Comment PROMPT and it's in your DMs

## Prompt payload (ManyChat DM)
MISSED-CALL-TO-BOOKED-APPOINTMENT SCRIPT

Use this as a text/SMS auto-reply template whenever your business misses an incoming call. Paste it into your phone system, CRM, or automation tool (or send manually) within 5 minutes of the missed call — speed is what makes this work.

---

STEP 1 — Immediate text reply (send within 5 min of a missed call):

"Hi [First Name], sorry we missed your call at [Business Name]! We'd love to help. What are you looking to book or ask about? Reply here and we'll get you sorted, or grab a time that works for you: [booking link]"

---

STEP 2 — If no reply within 2 hours, send this follow-up:

"Hi [First Name], just checking in — we don't want you to have to wait. Here's our booking link if it's easier: [booking link]. Or reply STOP if you'd rather we not follow up."

---

STEP 3 — If they reply with a question instead of booking, use this AI prompt to draft your response:

"You are the friendly front-desk assistant for [Business Name], a [type of business]. A potential customer just texted asking: '[paste their question]'. Write a warm, concise reply (under 60 words) that answers their question, reassures them we can help, and includes one clear next step: booking a time using this link [booking link]. Keep the tone helpful and human, not salesy."

---

WHY THIS WORKS
- Most leads book with whoever responds first — not whoever is "best."
- A 5-minute reply window beats a same-day callback almost every time.
- The two-step follow-up catches people who got busy but still want to book.

This is the exact logic Strumode installs as a live automation — every missed call triggers this sequence automatically, with no one at the front desk lifting a finger.
