## Caption
Every boutique owner knows the feeling: a customer loved what they bought, walked out happy, and you never heard from them again. No review. No repeat visit. Nothing.

There's a simple message you can send within an hour of a sale that actually gets people to leave a review — without sounding needy or robotic.

We use versions of this for retail and service clients every week. Today we're handing you the exact script.

Comment PROMPT and it's in your DMs.

## Prompt payload (ManyChat DM)
THE POST-PURCHASE REVIEW REQUEST — send within 1 hour of a sale/appointment

Use this as a text or email template. Fill in the bracketed parts, send as-is.

---

SUBJECT (if email): Quick thing, [First Name]?

MESSAGE:
Hi [First Name], it's [Your Name] from [Business Name]. Thanks again for coming in today — hope you love the [item/service purchased].

If you have 30 seconds, a review would mean a lot to us. It's how other people in [city/neighborhood] find us.

[Review link]

If anything wasn't perfect, just reply here first — I'll make it right before you post anything.

Thanks again,
[Your Name]

---

WHY THIS WORKS
- Sent within an hour, while the good feeling is still fresh — waiting a day cuts response rates fast
- Names the specific item/service, so it doesn't read like a mass blast
- Gives an easy out ("reply here first") which lowers the odds of a bad review going public and often turns a complaint into a fix instead
- One link, one ask — no explaining what a review is or why it matters

BONUS — THE 3-DAY FOLLOW-UP (for anyone who didn't respond)
"Hey [First Name], no worries if you're busy — if you get a sec, we'd still love your feedback: [Review link]. Either way, thanks for your business."

HOW TO AUTOMATE THIS
This works best when it's automatic and instant — not something you remember to do between customers. A simple setup: your point-of-sale or booking system triggers a text the moment a sale closes, using the customer's name and item automatically. That's the kind of thing we build into the Strumode system for retail and service businesses, so review requests go out every single time, with zero manual work.
