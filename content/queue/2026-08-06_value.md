## Caption
That client who "forgot" their appointment? They didn't forget. Nobody reminded them right.

Most no-show follow-ups sound like a robot or a guilt trip. There's a way to write them so clients actually rebook — same day, no awkwardness, no chasing.

We've got the exact script small business owners are using to fill last-minute cancellations without lifting a finger.

Comment PROMPT and it's in your DMs.

## Prompt payload (ManyChat DM)
NO-SHOW & CANCELLATION RECOVERY SCRIPT
A ready-to-use prompt + text template for filling gaps in your schedule fast.

STEP 1 — Copy this prompt into ChatGPT (or any AI chat tool):

"You are helping a [type of business, e.g. hair salon / dental office / dog groomer] owner write a short text message to a client who just canceled or no-showed an appointment. The message should:
- Be warm, not passive-aggressive
- Assume good intent (something came up, not that they're flaky)
- Offer 2 easy ways to rebook without back-and-forth (e.g. 'reply with a day that works' or 'here's my booking link')
- Include one soft incentive if the slot is rebooked within 48 hours (like priority scheduling, not a discount)
- Be under 40 words
- Sound like a real person texting, not a business

Write 3 versions: one for a no-show, one for a same-day cancellation, and one for a client who cancels repeatedly."

STEP 2 — Save all 3 versions in your Notes app or CRM as templates.

STEP 3 — The moment a cancellation hits your calendar, send the matching template within 15 minutes. Speed is what fills the slot — most owners wait a day and lose it.

BONUS AUTOMATION TIP:
If you use any booking software (Calendly, Square, Fresha, Acuity, etc.), most can auto-trigger a text the second a slot opens. Strumode installs this so it fires automatically — no copy-pasting, no remembering. Your calendar fills itself while you're actually working.
