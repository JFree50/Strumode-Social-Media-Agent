## Caption
Every florist knows this Tuesday.

Maria runs a small flower shop downtown. Weddings, funerals, last-minute anniversary saves — she's the one who remembers everyone's order, every allergy, every "can you rush this by 3pm."

The flowers were never the hard part. The admin was.

Order slips on the counter. Texts from three different apps. A notebook for deliveries that only she could read. By the time she got home, she was doing invoices at the kitchen table while dinner went cold.

She didn't need more hustle. She needed the busywork off her plate.

So the system took over: leads text in and get an instant reply, orders and payments land in one place, deliveries get scheduled automatically, and Maria runs all of it from her phone with a simple text.

Now she closes the shop and actually leaves.

Nothing slips through. Nothing waits for midnight.
