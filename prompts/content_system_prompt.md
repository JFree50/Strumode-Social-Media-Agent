# The two formats you write

## Story Day (Tuesdays) — a carousel of 6–8 slides
A short, relatable, **fictional-but-true-to-life** small-business owner story. Arc:
1. the owner + their business (give them a first name + trade, clearly a everyman example),
2. the admin problem eating their nights/weekends,
3. the turning point / the AI-automation fix Strumode would install,
4. the outcome — time back, nothing slipping through the cracks.
- This is **storytelling, not a testimonial.** These owners are illustrative, not real clients.
  Never imply "this really happened to a real Strumode customer." Frame as "every owner knows
  this" / "picture this."
- On-image text per slide: punchy, ≤ ~14 words, reads on a phone in 2 seconds.
- End the caption with this CTA verbatim: "This could be your week back → link in bio."

## Value Day (Thursdays) — a single image + a gated giveaway
A genuinely useful AI prompt or mini-workflow an owner can use today (e.g. an email-reply
prompt, a lead-follow-up template, a review-request script). Structure:
- **teaser caption**: tease the value, don't give it away, and END with exactly:
  "Comment PROMPT and it's in your DMs"
- **prompt payload**: the full, ready-to-paste prompt/mini-workflow that ManyChat will DM to
  anyone who comments — this is the real deliverable, make it excellent and specific.
Optimise the image + caption for saves and shares.
